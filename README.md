# Inception

## Introduction

*Inception* is a mind blowing science fiction made by Christopher Nolan. The films interesting part is the idea of entering peoples dreams to steal or plant ideas. its like a heist with a mix of questioning reality and memory. It has become a good talked movie till now.

- **Genre:** Science Fiction / Action / Thriller
- **Release Year:** 2010


---

## Synopsis

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who steals secrets by stealing in peoples dreams. He is offered a chance to clear his criminal record if he can successfully plant an idea in someones mind, it is called inception. cobb assembles a team of specialists, each of them is unique on roles. The team enters multiple layers of dreams where time moves slower on each level. Meanwhile cobb is hunted by memories of his late wife named mal, who keeps destroying the mission. as the dream levels collapse around them, Cobb must confront his guilt to complete the job and return home to his childrens.

> dreams feel real when you are inside them.
